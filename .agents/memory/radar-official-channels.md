---
name: Radar official channels
description: Verified access characteristics and attribution rules for the daily official Radar sources.
---

Use the official Ditjen Bina Konstruksi RSS as the permitted alternate channel for Kementerian PU and LPJK-related updates, and use SPSE ICB for LKPP tender packages plus dated tender announcements. Attribute the LPJK stream to Ditjen Bina Konstruksi, the actual publisher, rather than representing it as an independent LPJK feed.

**Why:** The direct PU and LPJK sites can reject automated requests even when their public pages are accessible. The public LKPP news site can also close the server-side TLS connection, while SPSE ICB is a published LKPP tender channel that responds to the runtime.

**How to apply:** Keep article URLs restricted to their configured official host. Treat SPSE download schedules as schedules, not publication dates; only dated SPSE announcements should populate an article publication date.
